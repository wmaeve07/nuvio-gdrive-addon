Get Your Google Credentials

Authorize the addon to read your Google Drive files:

A. Create OAuth Client:
1. Go to Google Cloud Console.
2. Create a New Project.
3. Go to APIs & Services > Library, search for Google Drive API, and Enable it.
4. Go to APIs & Services > Credentials. Click Create Credentials > OAuth client ID.
5. Application type: Web application. Name: Nuvio GDrive.
6. Under Authorized redirect URIs, paste this exact URL:
https://wmaeve07.github.io/nuvio-gdrive-addon/setup.html
7. Click Create. Copy your Client ID and Client Secret.
B. Add Test User:
1. Open the OAuth Consent Screen.
2. Enter App name = "Nuvio GDrive" and fill the email fields.
3. On the Test users screen, click + ADD USERS and enter your Gmail address.
⚠️ Skipping this causes an immediate "Access Blocked" authorization error.

Keep Token Active (Publish App)

Publish your app to prevent Google from expiring your refresh token every 7 days:

A. Fill in App Domain fields:
1. Go to the OAuth Branding Page (APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen → Branding).
2. Paste the URLs below into each required field:
https://nuvio.tv
https://nuvio.tv
https://nuvio.tv
nuvio.tv
3. Click Save at the bottom of the page.

B. Publish the app:
1. Click the Audience tab in the left sidebar.
2. Click Publish App and confirm when prompted.

Deploy & 1-Click Install
Deploy to Cloudflare Workers:
Open Cloudflare to create and save your worker.
🚀 Open Cloudflare Workers Dashboard
1. Click Create Worker.
2. Click Deploy, then select Edit Code.
3. Clear the editor, paste your copied code from Step 2, and click Save and Deploy.
4. Copy the URL assigned to your worker (e.g., https://my-addon.yourname.workers.dev).
